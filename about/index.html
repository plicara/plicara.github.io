about the lab

Good questions.
Experiments you can inspect.

plicara is an independent AI research lab. I study how systems behave, what makes them useful, and how to tell when they work.

What I am curious about

01. questions

Evaluation

What does a benchmark actually measure? Where does success on a test stop predicting success in use?

Systems around models

How do tools, interfaces and the information available to a model change what it can do?

How I investigate

02. practice

Start with a concrete question and a simple baseline. Build an experiment, inspect the failures, and revise the explanation when the evidence changes. State the limits alongside the result.

What I share

03. work in the open

Research articles, benchmarks and small tools that help others investigate the same questions. I aim to publish the evidence needed to check a claim, and to name the gaps when that evidence is incomplete.
